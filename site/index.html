One profile

A profile is the whole PC, not just a monitor layout.

Arrange everything the way you like it once, save it, and every switch brings back all of it. These are the five tabs of a profile in the app.

Displays

  • Which monitors are on, their layout and the main display
  • Resolution, refresh rate and HDR per monitor
  • NVIDIA Surround on or off, bezel correction kept
  • Optional screens, like a spacedesk tablet

Audio

  • Output and microphone, each with its volume
  • Separate devices for calls, e.g. for Discord
  • Game sound not lowered during calls
  • A monitor's HDMI sound is waited for

Apps

  • Start SimHub, Crew Chief or anything else
  • Close what the rig doesn't need
  • Wait for the previous app's window, or for a USB device

Triggers

  • USB rule: wheelbase and pedals on or off
  • Hotkey, desktop shortcut, rigshift:// link
  • Command line with exit codes

Behavior

  • Keep the PC awake while the rig is active
  • Its own desktop icon layout
  • Switch without asking, when you want that

Ways in

Switch the way your rig is built.

The wheelbase is the one no other display switcher offers. The others are there for the days you sit down without it.

The wheelbase itself

Pick your wheelbase and pedals once. When all devices of the rule are on, RigShift switches to the rig; when they have been off for the time you set, it goes back to the desk. It waits while the PC is locked and ignores a short USB dropout.

Hotkey

One per profile and game, plus one key that goes back and forth.

Stream Deck

A hotkey, a desktop shortcut or rigshift://apply/Rig as a website action.

Tray

Your profiles with their layout and your games, one click away.

Safety net

You never have to reboot to see your desk again.

Black screens are what people hate most about display switchers. This is what happens on every switch.

  1. CheckEvery display the profile needs is there. A missing one is named before anything changes.
  2. Wake and waitSleeping monitors are woken; a slow G9 gets the seconds it needs.
  3. One stepWindows receives the whole layout at once – it works, or nothing changes.
  4. CountdownKeep it with Enter or any button on the wheel, button box or controller.
  5. No answer, no pictureThe old layout and sound come back on their own.

Crash in the middle? The next start restores the layout from before. Screen still dark? Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D turns every display on, from anywhere.

Games

One click for the whole evening.

A game entry wraps a session around your sim. Installed games from Steam, Epic, EA, Xbox and iRacing are found on their own – nothing to type.

Before

  1. Switch to the game's profile
  2. Start Fanatec, SimHub, Crew Chief – in order, waiting for the wheelbase
  3. Place their windows where you saved them

During

  1. Launch the sim through its launcher
  2. Follow it, even through a launcher that stays open

Started the sim from Steam yourself? RigShift notices and does the same.

After

  1. Close the tools the session started
  2. Switch back to the desk, or stay – your choice

Trust

Check it instead of trusting it.

RigShift is a free hobby project and not code-signed, so Windows asks the first time: More info → Run anyway. Here is what you can check yourself.

Is it safe?

  • All source on GitHub, MIT licence. The only network request asks GitHub for updates – privacy.
  • VirusTotal scan linked on every release.
  • Built from this source, provable with a GitHub attestation: gh attestation verify RigShift-win-Setup.exe -R ManuelStaggl/RigShift
  • Checksums and SBOM in every release; each one is installed over the previous version and checked before it goes out.

Windows 11's Smart App Control blocks unsigned apps without a "run anyway". The download is about 80 MB because it brings its own .NET runtime; updates are a few MB.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does it work with AMD or Intel graphics?

It should: displays are switched through the plain Windows display API every driver supports. I only have NVIDIA to test on, so a hardware report helps. AMD Eyefinity is not supported.

What if a monitor is still asleep, or connected through spacedesk or a KVM?

Sleeping monitors are woken and waited for. Mark a screen as optional and the switch goes ahead without it. RigShift can only switch displays Windows sees at that moment.

Does it help with the field of view?

Yes: the field-of-view page takes your monitor's real size, your distance and the rig layout, and gives the value each of 18 sims wants – degrees, a multiplier, a slider or radians.

VR?

Audio and apps work in a VR profile, and a USB rule reacts to any USB device, a headset's link box included. The headset itself is not part of the layout, and I haven't tested a VR setup.

Does it need admin rights or the internet?

No admin rights: it installs for your user. It only talks to GitHub to check for updates. No account, no telemetry.

How do I get rid of it?

Windows Settings → Apps → RigShift → Uninstall. That also removes the desktop shortcuts it created. Your profiles stay in %AppData%\RigShift until you delete the folder.
